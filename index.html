Cycle Diary

A private period tracker for iPhone and iPad · by Sina Apps

Privacy Policy · Support

Cycle Diary is a private period tracker for iPhone and iPad. There is no account and nothing to sign up for — what you enter stays on your device, and syncs only to your own private iCloud storage.

What it does

  • Track periods, flow, moods, symptoms and notes on a simple calendar.
  • See predictions for your next period, and optionally your fertile window.
  • Reminders before your cycle is due, as local notifications.
  • Optional app lock with a PIN or Face ID.
  • Export and import your data as CSV.
  • Available in 50 languages.

Privacy

No account. No servers of ours. No advertising, no analytics, no tracking. Your entries sync through your own Apple Account, encrypted so that only you can read them. The full policy is on the Privacy Policy page.
